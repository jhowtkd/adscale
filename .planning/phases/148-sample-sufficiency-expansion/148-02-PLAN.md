# Plan 148-02 - Sample-Guidance Rerun and Claim-State Update

## Goal

Rerun calibration and release evidence after sample expansion or blocker capture, then update the claims state based on actual human decisions and sample guidance.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Calibration rerun reflects the latest sample state.
- Evidence rebuild/check passes with truthful status.
- `agreementRate` stays `null` while `additionalNeeded > 0`.
- If `additionalNeeded=0`, agreement and mismatch metrics use only comparable rows with both system and human verdicts.
- `148-SAMPLE-GATE.md` states:
  - current decision count;
  - required decision count;
  - additional needed;
  - source composition;
  - allowed and forbidden claims.
- Phase 148 verification syncs ROADMAP/STATE/REQUIREMENTS without hiding blockers.

## Tasks

### 148-02-02 - Assert claim state

**Requirements:** SAMPLE-02, SAMPLE-03

## Stop Conditions

- Do not unlock `agreementRate` while `additionalNeeded > 0`.
- Do not mark sample sufficient from rows without human decisions.
- Do not proceed to agreement calibration if comparable rows are still zero.
- Do not remove source caveats from evidence or audit.
