Discretion defaults (no CONTEXT.md — per RESEARCH):
- Bucket granularity: ISO week (UTC) of `evaluation.createdAt` — NOT `selectedAt`
- Regression threshold: visual mean drop ≥ 5 points between last two populated buckets where both `count >= TREND_SLICE_MIN`
- Stale: `latestEvaluatedAt > capturedAt` (ISO strings compared)
- Learning impact per bucket: `ok | insufficient_sample` based on learned/non-learned arm counts ≥ `TREND_SLICE_MIN`
- Live human corpus only — never mix fixture metrics into trend series
