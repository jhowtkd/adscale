# Plan 147-02 - Calibration Rerun, Evidence Refresh and Human-Needed Audit

## Goal

Rerun the calibration and release evidence after the decision session, then document the truthful outcome: updated comparable metrics if decisions exist, or explicit `human_needed` if they do not.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Calibration rerun completes against live mode.
- Metrics reflect persisted decisions:
  - `humanDecisionCount`;
  - `missingHumanDecisionCount`;
  - `comparableCount`;
  - `mismatchReasonCounts`.
- Release evidence rebuilds from the rerun calibration.
- Evidence checker passes with truthful status.
- Verification states whether Phase 147 advanced the operator loop or remains blocked by missing decisions.
- No agreement or quality claim appears while sample guidance blocks it.
