# Plan 145-02 - Mismatch Taxonomy and Comparable-Row Agreement Metrics

## Goal

Re-run calibration after decisions and turn the result into actionable agreement/mismatch evidence while preserving sample-size and synthetic-fixture caveats.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Mismatch reasons are normalized into actionable buckets.
- Calibration rerun consumes recorded decisions from `output_decision_events` or explicit normalized artifact input.
- Metrics use only comparable rows with both system verdict and human decision.
- `missingHumanDecisionCount` reflects any remaining undecided rows.
- `agreementRate` is not used as a quality claim while sample guidance requires more decisions.
- Phase 146 receives a clear handoff: ok to refresh evidence, but claims remain withheld unless sample guidance clears.
