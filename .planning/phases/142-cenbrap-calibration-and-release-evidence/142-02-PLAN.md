# Plan 142-02 - Release Evidence, Audit and Milestone Closure

## Goal

Turn the Cenbrap calibration run into release evidence and close v12.7 honestly: factual fidelity, art-direction agreement and sample sufficiency must remain separate.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `142-EVIDENCE.json` reports agreement, approved-invalid prevention, `sem_opiniao` detection, export-block separation and remaining gaps.
- Evidence status is `insufficient_sample` when thresholds are not met.
- Movement/agreement claims are null or withheld when sample guidance has `additionalNeeded > 0`.
- Factual/export safety metrics are separate from art-direction quality metrics.
- v12.7 milestone audit states whether the milestone is passed, human-needed, or shipped with accepted operational gap.
- ROADMAP/STATE/REQUIREMENTS are synchronized only after evidence and audit are generated.
