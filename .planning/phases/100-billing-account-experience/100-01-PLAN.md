# Plan 100-01: Billing Account Experience

## Objective

Make Billing settings the accurate account surface for paid, trial, past-due, canceled, beta, and no-access workspaces.

## Must Haves

- Trial end or next renewal is visible when supplied by the normalized status contract.
- Past-due and canceled banners use the correct recovery action.
- Credit grant history displays source, amount, and date from the ledger API.
- PT-BR and EN copy clearly distinguish beta, paid, and no-access states.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Complete billing data contracts

Align `/api/billing/status`, `/api/billing/history`, `use-billing.ts`, and their tests around dates, status, access kind, and grant history.

### Task 2: Refactor BillingTab states

Render explicit account-state sections and actions in `components/settings/BillingTab.tsx`, keeping the internal forecast calculator separate from user credit history.

### Task 3: Localize and verify

Add PT-BR/EN strings and tests for every account state, empty history, loading/error behavior, portal, and checkout actions.

## Verification

- Billing route, hook, component, and translation tests pass.
- Browser smoke covers trial/active, beta, and past-due representative states.
