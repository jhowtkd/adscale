# Plan 152-01 - Brand Taste Profile Read Model

## Goal

Aggregate calibration signals into an inspectable brand taste profile with separated positive, rejection, and quase patterns.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `buildBrandTasteProfile()` produces a profile from signals for a `clientProfileId`.
- Positive, rejection, and `quase` patterns are grouped separately with counts and sample derivation ids.
- Profile includes decision and comparable counts.
