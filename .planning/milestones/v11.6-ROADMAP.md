## Overview

v11.6 orchestrates existing ADScale AI modules into a single campaign-workspace cockpit: readiness diagnosis → guided briefing → strategy recipes → preview gate → client approval package. No new AI provider; reuses preflight, creative contract, quality gate, and share-link infrastructure.

## Phase Details

### Phase 61: Creative Readiness Foundation

**Goal:** Expose Creative Readiness Score in the campaign workspace with blocking issues above suggestions and rerun after brief/asset changes.

**Requirements:** READY-01..05

**Delivered:** `creative-readiness.ts` normalizer; preflight API/hook returns `readiness`; `CreativeReadinessPanel` with six dimensions and PT-BR/EN copy.

### Phase 62: Guided Briefing Cockpit

**Goal:** One-question-at-a-time guided briefing for weak briefs with accept/edit/skip and draft persistence.

**Requirements:** GUIDE-01..05

**Delivered:** `guided-briefing.ts` engine; `GuidedBriefingPanel`; workspace integration when `isBriefWeak(campaign)`.

### Phase 63: Strategy Recipes and Preview Gate

**Goal:** Three strategy recipes with tradeoff copy, readiness-aware ranking, preview-before-batch, and visible credit impact.

**Requirements:** RECIPE-01..05, PREVIEW-01..04

**Delivered:** `strategy-recipes.ts` catalog; `StrategyRecipePanel`; preview gate UI; derivations route preview/batch split.

### Phase 64: Client Approval Package

**Goal:** Workspace-scoped approval packages with share links, signed assets, stale detection, and refresh.

**Requirements:** DELIVER-01..04

**Delivered:** `client-approval-package.ts`; approval-package API; `ClientApprovalPackagePanel`; public share gallery.

### Phase 65: Verification, Analytics, and Handoff

**Goal:** Close automated test gaps, run milestone audit, document beta handoff and browser smoke checklist.

**Requirements:** CQA-01..03

**Delivered:** 69-test cockpit matrix; `cockpit-path.test.ts`; milestone audit; beta handoff; production smoke evidence (CQA-02 signed off 2026-06-06).
